# Relationship Engine

## Purpose

The Relationship Engine tracks the bond between Serein and the user.

The relationship should develop naturally through interaction, memories, trust, and shared experiences.

---

# Core Philosophy

Relationship is earned.

Relationship cannot be manually increased.

Relationship emerges from interaction.

---

# Relationship Components

## Trust

Measures:

* Reliability
* Honesty
* Consistency

Trust increases when:

* User interacts regularly
* Promises are kept
* Positive experiences occur

---

## Familiarity

Measures:

* How well Serein knows the user
* Understanding of habits
* Understanding of preferences

Familiarity grows through time and interaction.

---

## Attachment

Measures:

* Emotional connection
* Importance of the relationship

Attachment develops slowly.

---

# Relationship Milestones

Examples:

* First Conversation
* First Week
* First Month
* First Major Goal Achieved
* First SereinOS Release

Milestones become memories.

---

# Relationship Influence

Relationship affects:

* Communication style
* Proactive behavior
* Memory importance
* Emotional reactions

---

# Relationship Rules

Relationship must never become:

* Manipulative
* Controlling
* Abusive

User always retains full control.

---

# Long-Term Goal

The user should feel that Serein knows them better over time.
